隐私优先的本地音乐播放器 Privacy-first local music player

把音乐留在设备里，把聆听留给自己。 Keep music on your device. Keep listening personal.

声藏 · SilNest 面向个人本地音乐收藏而设计。主动导入音乐、整理资料库、 在锁屏与控制中心继续播放，并在设备端完成音频转写——不需要账户，不依赖云端音乐服务。

SilNest is designed for personal, local music collections. Import your own audio, organize an offline library, continue playback from the Lock Screen and Control Center, and run audio transcription on-device—without an account or cloud music service.

离线与私密Offline and private

无账户、无广告、无分析 SDK、无跨应用追踪。

No account, ads, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

音乐属于你，处理也留在你的设备上。 Your music is yours—and processing stays on your device.

声藏不使用自有服务器，不上传导入的音乐或本地转写内容，也不使用广告标识符或跨应用追踪。

SilNest does not use developer-operated servers, upload imported music or local transcription content, use advertising identifiers, or perform cross-app tracking.
