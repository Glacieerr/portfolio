隐私政策 Privacy Policy

本政策说明声藏 · SilNest 如何处理用户主动导入的音乐、本地资料与技术支持通信。

This policy explains how SilNest handles user-imported music, local app data, and support communications.

核心结论Key Summary

声藏不要求账户，不使用开发者服务器，不上传用户导入的音乐或本地转写内容，不使用广告、分析 SDK、广告标识符或跨应用追踪。音乐及相关信息在用户设备上处理。

SilNest requires no account, uses no developer-operated server, does not upload imported music or local transcription content, and does not use ads, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking. Music and related information are processed on the user's device.

1. 适用范围与概览1. Scope and Overview

本隐私政策适用于由 Canyuan Ye 开发的 iOS 应用“声藏 · SilNest”（以下简称“本应用”）。本应用是一款面向用户自有本地音乐文件的播放器与资料库工具。

本应用不提供账户系统，不运营用于保存用户音乐或个人资料的服务器，也不要求用户向开发者提交个人信息才能使用核心功能。

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS app “声藏 · SilNest” (“the App”), developed by Canyuan Ye. The App is a player and library tool for music files owned or controlled by the user.

The App does not provide an account system, operate servers for storing user music or profiles, or require personal information to use its core features.

2. 用户导入的音乐与相关内容2. User-Imported Music and Related Content

用户通过 iOS 提供的系统文件选择界面主动选择并导入音频文件。本应用仅访问用户明确选择的文件，而不会任意浏览设备上的全部文件。

为建立本地音乐资料库，本应用可能在设备上读取或处理：

  • 音频文件本身；
  • 文件名、文件大小和文件修改时间等必要的文件属性；
  • 歌曲名称、歌手、专辑等嵌入式元数据；
  • 专辑封面；
  • 嵌入式或与本地文件关联的歌词。

用户导入的文件可能被复制到本应用的私有存储空间，以便建立本地资料库、保持文件可用性并提供播放功能。上述内容不会上传至开发者服务器或第三方音乐服务。

Users select and import audio files through the system file picker provided by iOS. The App accesses only files explicitly selected by the user and does not freely browse all files on the device.

To build the local music library, the App may read or process on the device:

  • the audio file itself;
  • necessary file properties such as file name, size, and modification time;
  • embedded metadata such as song title, artist, and album;
  • album artwork; and
  • embedded lyrics or lyrics associated with local files.

Imported files may be copied into the App's private storage to maintain the local library, preserve file availability, and support playback. This content is not uploaded to developer-operated servers or third-party music services.

3. 设备端音频转写3. On-device Audio Transcription

本应用可以使用 SwiftWhisper 与 whisper.cpp 对用户已导入的音频进行转写。转写处理在用户设备本地完成，不会将音频发送至外部 AI API 或远程转写服务。

转写结果保存在本应用的本地存储中，并由用户在本应用内使用或删除。转写准确度可能受音频质量、语言、背景声音和设备性能影响。

The App may use SwiftWhisper and whisper.cpp to transcribe audio already imported by the user. Transcription runs locally on the user's device. Audio is not sent to an external AI API or remote transcription service.

Transcription results are stored locally by the App and can be used or deleted within the App. Accuracy may vary based on audio quality, language, background sound, and device performance.

4. 数据收集、服务器、分析和追踪4. Data Collection, Servers, Analytics, and Tracking

截至本政策生效日期，本应用：

  • 不收集或上传用户的音乐文件、封面、歌词或本地转写内容；
  • 不使用开发者自有服务器、数据库或云函数保存用户内容；
  • 不使用 iCloud 或 CloudKit 同步用户音乐资料库；
  • 不使用分析 SDK 或崩溃报告 SDK；
  • 不展示广告；
  • 不使用 IDFA 或其他广告标识符；
  • 不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪；
  • 不调用在线歌词、在线封面、第三方音乐数据库或外部 AI API。

As of the effective date of this policy, the App:

  • does not collect or upload music files, artwork, lyrics, or local transcription content;
  • does not use developer-operated servers, databases, or cloud functions to store user content;
  • does not use iCloud or CloudKit to synchronize the user's music library;
  • does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • does not display advertising;
  • does not use IDFA or other advertising identifiers;
  • does not track users across apps or websites; and
  • does not call online lyrics, artwork, third-party music databases, or external AI APIs.

5. 系统权限与平台功能5. System Permissions and Platform Features

本应用使用系统文件选择界面，由用户主动选择需要导入的文件。本应用不请求麦克风、相机、照片资料库、位置、通知、本地网络或 App Tracking Transparency 权限。

本应用启用 iOS 后台音频能力，以便在应用进入后台时继续播放，并通过锁屏界面、控制中心和兼容的系统媒体界面提供播放信息与控制。这些系统媒体功能不属于推送通知。

本应用可以通过 iOS 系统音频路由向兼容的耳机、扬声器或 AirPlay 设备播放音频。本应用不会自行扫描、识别或连接附近的蓝牙设备，也不请求 Core Bluetooth 权限。

The App uses the system file picker, allowing users to choose files for import. The App does not request microphone, camera, photo library, location, notification, local network, or App Tracking Transparency permissions.

The App enables the iOS background-audio capability so playback can continue while the App is in the background, with playback information and controls available on the Lock Screen, Control Center, and compatible system media surfaces. These media features are not push notifications.

The App can play audio through iOS system audio routing to compatible headphones, speakers, or AirPlay devices. It does not independently scan for, identify, or connect to nearby Bluetooth devices and does not request Core Bluetooth access.

6. 本地数据保存与删除6. Local Data Retention and Deletion

导入的音乐副本、音乐资料、歌词、设置与转写结果保存在用户设备上的本应用私有存储中，直至用户在本应用内删除相关内容，或卸载本应用。

卸载本应用通常会删除其应用容器中的本地数据。根据用户的设备备份、迁移或恢复设置，iOS 或用户选择的备份服务可能保留应用数据的备份副本；此类系统级备份由平台和用户设置控制。

Imported music copies, library information, lyrics, settings, and transcription results remain in the App's private on-device storage until the user deletes the relevant content within the App or uninstalls the App.

Uninstalling the App normally removes local data in its app container. Depending on device backup, migration, or restore settings, iOS or a backup service selected by the user may retain backup copies. Such system-level backups are controlled by the platform and the user's settings.

7. 技术支持通信7. Support Communications

本网站不提供在线表单，也不会自动收集问题反馈。当用户主动通过电子邮件联系开发者时，开发者会收到用户选择提供的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件。

这些信息仅用于回复支持请求、调查问题、改进本应用或处理用户提出的其他事项。除非法律要求，或为发送和接收电子邮件而由邮件服务提供商进行必要处理，开发者不会出售这些信息，也不会将其用于广告或跨服务追踪。

请不要在支持邮件中发送音乐文件、歌词文件、账户密码、身份证件或其他不必要的敏感信息。

This website does not provide an online submission form and does not automatically collect feedback. When a user voluntarily contacts the developer by email, the developer receives the email address, message content, and attachments the user chooses to provide.

This information is used only to respond to support requests, investigate issues, improve the App, or address other matters raised by the user. It is not sold or used for advertising or cross-service tracking, except for processing required by email service providers to transmit and receive the communication or where required by law.

Do not send music files, lyric files, account passwords, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information in support emails.

8. 儿童隐私8. Children's Privacy

本应用不面向儿童收集个人信息，也不提供账户、社交网络、广告或在线用户内容服务。如果监护人认为儿童通过支持邮件提供了不必要的个人信息，可以联系开发者请求删除相关通信记录。

The App does not collect personal information from children and does not provide accounts, social networking, advertising, or online user-content services. A guardian who believes a child provided unnecessary personal information in a support email may contact the developer to request deletion of the relevant correspondence.

9. 安全说明9. Security

本应用通过 iOS 应用沙盒与系统文件选择机制限制数据访问范围，并以本地处理方式减少音乐内容传输风险。但任何设备或软件系统都无法保证绝对安全。用户应保护设备解锁凭证，并及时安装系统安全更新。

The App relies on the iOS app sandbox and system file picker to limit access and uses local processing to reduce risks associated with transmitting music content. No device or software system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect device credentials and install system security updates.

10. 本政策的更新10. Changes to This Policy

如果本应用的数据处理方式、功能或第三方依赖发生变化，本政策可能更新。更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并标明新的生效日期或最后更新日期。若变化影响 App Store 隐私披露，开发者也会相应更新 App Store Connect 中的信息。

This policy may be updated if the App's data practices, features, or third-party dependencies change. The revised policy will be published on this page with a new effective or last-updated date. If a change affects App Store privacy disclosures, the developer will also update the corresponding information in App Store Connect.
