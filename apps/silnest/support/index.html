发送问题报告前Before Sending a Bug Report

以下信息通常能显著提高问题定位效率。

The following details usually make troubleshooting much faster.

  • 应用版本和构建版本；
  • iOS 版本与设备型号；
  • 问题发生前执行的操作；
  • 是否能够稳定复现；
  • 预期结果与实际结果；
  • 不包含音乐内容或敏感信息的截图。
  • App version and build number;
  • iOS version and device model;
  • actions performed before the issue occurred;
  • whether the issue is consistently reproducible;
  • expected and actual behavior; and
  • screenshots that do not reveal music content or sensitive information.
请不要发送音乐文件、歌词文件、账户密码、身份证件或其他不必要的敏感内容。

常见问题Frequently Asked Questions

为什么某个音乐文件无法导入或播放？Why can't a music file be imported or played?

请确认文件可由 iOS 文件系统访问、文件没有损坏，并且不是受数字版权保护而禁止第三方播放的内容。尝试从系统“文件”应用重新选择该文件。若问题持续，请在报告中注明文件扩展名、文件来源和错误表现，但不要附加音乐文件本身。

Confirm that the file is accessible through the iOS file system, is not corrupted, and is not protected in a way that prevents third-party playback. Try selecting it again from the Files app. If the issue continues, include the file extension, source, and observed behavior—but do not attach the audio file itself.

为什么歌曲名称、歌手、专辑或封面显示不完整？Why are title, artist, album, or artwork missing?

声藏不会联网补全音乐信息。显示结果取决于文件中已有的嵌入式元数据和本地关联内容。可先使用可信的本地元数据工具检查音频文件是否包含完整信息，再重新导入。

SilNest does not fetch music information online. Displayed information depends on metadata embedded in the file and locally associated content. You can inspect or update the file with a trusted local metadata tool before importing it again.

为什么锁屏或控制中心没有显示正确的播放信息？Why is playback information missing from the Lock Screen or Control Center?

先确认歌曲正在声藏中播放，并尝试暂停后继续播放。若系统刚刚切换过音频输出设备，播放信息可能需要短暂时间更新。重新启动应用或设备也可能恢复系统媒体界面状态。

Confirm that a track is actively playing in SilNest, then pause and resume playback. The system media interface may take a moment to update after switching audio outputs. Restarting the App or device may also restore the system playback display.

应用进入后台后为什么停止播放？Why does playback stop in the background?

声藏使用 iOS 后台音频能力。请确认没有从多任务界面强制关闭应用，并检查是否有其他应用取得音频控制权。系统重启、音频路由变化或文件不可访问也可能中断播放。若问题可复现，请提交设备、系统和操作步骤。

SilNest uses the iOS background-audio capability. Confirm that the App was not force-quit and that another app did not take control of audio playback. System restarts, audio-route changes, or unavailable files may also interrupt playback. Submit device, system, and reproduction details if the issue is repeatable.

本地音频转写为什么很慢或不准确？Why is local transcription slow or inaccurate?

转写在设备上运行，速度会受到设备性能、音频时长、模型和可用资源影响。准确度还会受到语言、混响、伴奏、人声清晰度和背景声音影响。较长的处理过程可能增加耗电或使设备温度升高。

Transcription runs on the device, so speed depends on device performance, audio duration, the model, and available resources. Accuracy is affected by language, reverberation, instrumentation, vocal clarity, and background sound. Longer processing may increase battery use or device temperature.

声藏会上传我的音乐、歌词或转写结果吗？Does SilNest upload my music, lyrics, or transcription results?

不会。按照当前版本的设计，音乐、封面、歌词与转写结果均在设备本地处理和保存。详情请阅读隐私政策。

No. In the current version, music, artwork, lyrics, and transcription results are processed and stored locally on the device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

如何删除导入的音乐和转写内容？How do I delete imported music and transcription content?

使用应用内提供的删除或资料库管理功能移除相关内容。卸载应用通常会删除其私有应用容器中的本地数据；设备备份或迁移可能由 iOS 和用户设置单独管理。

Use the App's delete or library-management controls to remove the relevant content. Uninstalling the App normally removes data from its private app container; device backups or migrations may be managed separately by iOS and the user's settings.
